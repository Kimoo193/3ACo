FAQ

What does 3A deliver?

Electrical (wiring, boards, lighting, power), mechanical (HVAC, plumbing, fire fighting), low current (data networks, racks, fiber, CCTV, fire alarm, public address, access control, central satellite TV), smart systems, and maintenance contracts.

Do you supply equipment or install only?

Both. We supply, install, test and hand over ready to run, and we can also install client-supplied equipment.

Do you work outside Cairo?

Yes. We have delivered in the New Administrative Capital, Alexandria, Suez, El Alamein, Giza, Qena, Port Said and Hayat Karima villages, and serve private clients in Saudi Arabia and Kuwait.

Do you build management software?

Yes. 3A's software team builds CRM systems and management software for companies, factories and clinics, tailored to how each client works.

Do you offer maintenance contracts?

Yes. For example, our maintenance contract with the General Motors factory was renewed for the following period.

What do you need to prepare a quote?

Send drawings or a BOQ, the site location and your timeline over WhatsApp or the form below, and we will get back to you after reviewing them.

Do you work with government bodies and main contractors?

Yes. We have worked with government entities, the Administrative Capital company and ENPPI, and as a subcontractor to contractors such as Al-Jayed and Mountain Construction.
